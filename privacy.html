4. Live visitor statistics (Google Firebase) – only with your consent

The “Total Visitors” and “Online Now” display on the home page is loaded only after you click “Load stats”. Before that, no connection to Google is made.

This entry is deleted as soon as your connection ends. I do not link this data to you as a person.

The provider is Google Ireland Limited, Gordon House, Barrow Street, Dublin 4, Ireland, and Google LLC, USA. Google processes the data as a processor. Google LLC is certified under the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework. More information: Privacy and Security in Firebase.

Your browser additionally stores two entries: “stats-consent” in local storage (remembers your choice) and “counted” in session storage (so your visit is counted only once per session; deleted when you close the tab).

The legal basis is your consent, Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR and Section 25(1) TDDDG. Storing your choice itself is based on Section 25(2) no. 2 TDDDG. You can withdraw your consent at any time with effect for the future: use the “Forget my choice” link in the statistics box, or clear this website’s data in your browser. Processing carried out until then remains lawful.
